Prize draw terms

Last updated: 22 October 2026

1. Organiser

Konvo GmbH, registered in the Berlin Commercial Register under number HRB 257880 B, VAT ID DE366060427, with its registered office in Berlin (Germany). Contact: hola@konvoai.com.

2. Dates

  • Entry period: from 22 October 2026 to Friday 6 November 2026, 23:59 (CET, mainland Spain time).
  • Draw and winner announcement: Monday 9 November 2026.

3. Who can enter

  • Companies with an online store (e‑commerce) that are not Konvo customers on the date of entry, represented by a person aged 18 or over with authority to act on their behalf.
  • The company is the entrant: one entry per store, even if several people attend the demo.
  • Employees of Konvo and of its launch partners may not enter.

4. How to enter

  1. Register at konvoai.com/k2 and book a Konvo 2.0 demo.
  2. Do the demo before 6 November 2026. Booking it is not enough: the entry is confirmed once the demo has taken place.
  3. Entry is free and carries no obligation to sign up for Konvo.

5. Prize

A single winner will receive:

  • Free use of Konvo from 27 November 2026 to 6 January 2027, on the plan Konvo considers appropriate for the store’s volume.
  • Implementation and 1:1 support from the Konvo team, so the service is up and running before 27 November.

The prize cannot be exchanged for cash or transferred to third parties. When the free period ends there is no automatic renewal: the winner is free to decide whether to continue with Konvo.

6. Audit for all entrants

Regardless of the draw, every store that does the demo receives a free customer service audit.

7. Draw and notification

  • The winner will be chosen at random from all valid entries on 9 November 2026. Two reserves will also be chosen.
  • The winner will be contacted by email and phone. If the winner does not respond within 3 working days or does not accept the prize, it passes to the first reserve.
  • With the winner’s consent, Konvo may publish the store’s name on its website and social media.

8. Existing customers: referral draw

Konvo customers can enter a separate draw for Konvo free over Black Friday and Christmas (from 27 November 2026 to 6 January 2027) by recommending another store. Each recommended store that does the demo before 6 November through the customer’s personal link gives them one entry. The draw takes place on 9 November 2026 under the same conditions as section 7.

9. Data protection

Controller: Konvo GmbH. Purpose: to manage entry, the demo and the draw and, if the entrant agrees, to send them marketing communications. Entrants can exercise their rights of access, rectification, erasure and objection by writing to hola@konvoai.com.

  • Konvo may disqualify entries that are fraudulent or do not comply with these terms.
